# Third-party excerpts

The graph, its structure, claims, grades, notes, search matrices and scene settings are licensed under CC BY 4.0 (see `LICENSE`).

Each `source` node also quotes a short excerpt from a third-party page, at most 300 characters, as a citation for the fact it supports. Each excerpt comes with the page's `url` and `title`, and the date it was read (`accessed`). These excerpts remain under the terms of their original publishers and are **not** relicensed by this dataset. Common cases:

- **Wikipedia** text is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Its excerpts are attributed by their article URL.
- **Wikisource, Perseus, CCEL and similar editions** of ancient authors are mostly public-domain translations, such as Whiston's Josephus. Some editions carry their own licences, given on the linked page.
- **Sefaria** texts carry the licence shown for each version on Sefaria.
- **Museum, excavation and reference pages** remain under their publishers' terms. The excerpts are short quotations for citation.

If you reuse the excerpts beyond citation, check the licence on the linked page. If you are a publisher and want an excerpt removed or changed, open an issue.

The base text, the World English Bible, is in the public domain.
